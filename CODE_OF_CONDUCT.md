# Community conduct

Moztopia Notifications welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, constructive and patient. Critique code and ideas rather than people; make space for questions and differing opinions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized content, impersonation, malicious links, spam and repeated disruptive behavior are not welcome. Do not publish other people's personal information, private messages, credentials or device tokens. Keep issues and Discussions relevant to this package.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock threads, warn participants, or restrict/block accounts according to severity and repeated behavior. Good-faith questions, disagreement and security research are welcome; follow the private security-reporting path for vulnerabilities.

Use GitHub's **Report content / Report abuse** controls for unsafe content or conduct that should not be posted publicly. For a routine discussion dispute, flag the specific public comment to maintainers without publishing private information. GitHub's platform safety reporting provides a private route; this project does not publish a separate confidential conduct mailbox.

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards fairly. Sponsorship, contributor history and repository permissions do not excuse abusive behavior.
